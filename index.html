Kebijakan Privasi

Siapa kami. Recording Kandang ("aplikasi") dikelola oleh Joshua, di Indonesia, kontak: joshua.hermanto97@gmail.com.

Yang disimpan aplikasi

  • Akun: alamat email dan nama Anda, untuk masuk.
  • Jika Anda memilih masuk dengan Google, Google memverifikasi Anda dan memberikan alamat email serta nama profil Anda kepada penyedia layanan masuk kami, Supabase. Anda juga dapat masuk dengan email dan kata sandi, lalu mengatur ulang kata sandi lewat kode email. Email dari aplikasi, seperti kode atur ulang kata sandi, dikirim melalui penyedia email kami, Brevo, yang menerima alamat email Anda untuk keperluan itu.
  • Catatan peternakan yang Anda isi: kandang, ayam, catatan harian, penimbangan, kesehatan dan diagnosis, stok, strain dan standarnya, serta orang yang Anda undang.
  • Laporan kunjungan: penilaian dan kesimpulan Technical Support (TS) atas peternakan, dan permintaan (perubahan strain, diagnosis) yang menunggu persetujuan pemilik peternakan.
  • Organisasi TS: nama organisasi, anggotanya (nama dan email), peternakan yang membagikan kandang kepadanya, kandang yang dipegang tiap TS, dan strain milik organisasi.
  • Logo organisasi, jika adminnya memilih: gambar dari galeri ponsel (aplikasi tidak memakai kamera), diperkecil di ponsel lalu diunggah. Logo disimpan di Supabase dan bisa dibuka siapa pun yang memegang tautannya; aplikasi menampilkannya kepada anggota organisasi dan peternakan yang bekerja sama dengannya.
  • Semua ini disimpan di ponsel Anda dan di penyedia database kami (Supabase, server di Singapura) agar ponsel tim Anda tetap sama.
  • File yang Anda impor (riwayat flok atau standar strain dari Excel) dibaca di ponsel; hanya barisnya yang disimpan. Laporan yang Anda ekspor (PDF, Excel) disimpan di tempat yang Anda pilih dan tidak diunggah.

Siapa yang bisa melihatnya

Hanya orang di peternakan Anda, sesuai peran yang diberikan pemilik: pengawas, pekerja dan TS hanya melihat kandang yang ditugaskan. Pemilik dapat mengatur apakah pengawas melihat laporan dan Benchmark. Pemilik boleh mengundang Technical Support (TS) (misalnya konsultan atau technical service perusahaan pakan) dengan kode TS; TS membaca catatan kandang yang ditugaskan dan mencetak laporannya tetapi tidak bisa mengubah catatan. Pemilik juga boleh bergabung ke organisasi TS dengan kode dari adminnya, memilih kandang mana yang boleh dilihat dan apakah flok sebelum dibagikan ikut terlihat; organisasi membaca kandang itu tetapi tidak bisa mengubah apa pun, adminnya menugaskan TS-nya ke kandang itu, dan admin membaca laporan kunjungan yang mereka tulis. Pemilik bisa berhenti membagikan kandang atau memutus organisasi kapan saja (Akun > Organisasi); organisasi tetap menyimpan yang dibagikan sampai hari itu. Pemilik bisa mengeluarkan TS kapan saja dari Anggota. Kami tidak menjual data Anda.

Permintaan video memberi tahu Google ID akun dan ID peternakan Anda, agar konfirmasi bertanda tangan dari Google sampai ke server kami dan orang yang tepat menerima koinnya. Koin juga bisa dibeli lewat Google Play oleh pemilik peternakan, TS atau admin organisasi, dan pemilik bisa membeli Pro untuk sebulan atau setahun: Google memproses pembayaran; kami hanya menerima token pembelian, yang kami periksa ke Google sebelum memberi koin atau Pro. Kami menyimpan catatan setiap koin yang didapat, dibeli dan dipakai, serta apa yang dibelinya.

Privacy Policy

Who we are. Recording Kandang ("the app") is operated by Joshua, based in Indonesia, contact: joshua.hermanto97@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • Account: your email address and name, used to sign in.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, Google authenticates you and shares your email address and profile name with our sign-in provider, Supabase. You can also sign in with email and password and reset a forgotten password by email code. Emails from the app, such as a password reset code, are sent through our email provider, Brevo, which receives your email address for that purpose.
  • Farm records you enter: houses, birds, daily sheets, weighings, health entries and diagnoses, stock, strains and their standards, and the people you invite.
  • Visit reports: a Technical Support (TS) person's verdict and conclusion on a farm, and the requests (a strain change, a diagnosis) that wait for the farm owner's approval.
  • TS organisations: the organisation's name, its members (name and email), the farms that share houses with it, which houses each TS is in charge of, and its own strains.
  • An organisation's logo, if its admin chooses one: a picture from the phone's gallery (the app does not use the camera), made small on the phone and uploaded. It is stored with Supabase and can be loaded by anyone holding its link; the app shows it to the organisation's people and to the farms that work with it.
  • These are stored on your phone and on our database provider (Supabase, servers in Singapore) so your team's phones stay in sync.
  • Files you import (an Excel flock history or a strain standard) are read on your phone; only the rows they contain are saved. Reports you export (PDF, Excel) are saved where you choose and are not uploaded.

Who can see it

Only the people on your farm, according to the role the farm owner gives them: supervisors, workers and TS see only assigned houses. The owner can choose whether a supervisor sees reports and Benchmark. An owner may invite a Technical Support (TS) person (for example a consultant or a feed company's technical service) with a TS code; the TS reads assigned farm records and prints reports but cannot change records. An owner may also join a TS organisation with a code its admin makes, choosing which houses it may read and whether it also sees the flocks before sharing; the organisation reads those houses but cannot change anything, its admin assigns its TS to them, and the admin reads the visit reports they write. The owner can stop sharing a house or disconnect the organisation at any time (Account > Organisations); it keeps what was shared up to that day. The owner can remove a TS at any time from People. We do not sell your data.

Notifications

If you turn on phone notifications in Settings, the app shows its own reminders (missing sheets, approvals, visit reports) on this phone. They are made on the phone; nothing is sent to a notification service.

Ads

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it you are asked for consent first. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Coins, videos and purchases

In the Shop anyone on a farm may watch a video to earn coins: an owner or a TS earns for themselves, other members earn for the farm's owner. The video request tells Google your account id and farm id so that Google's signed confirmation reaches our server and the right person is credited. Coins can also be bought through Google Play by a farm owner, a TS or an organisation's admin, and a farm owner can buy Pro for a month or a year: Google processes the payment; we receive only the purchase token, which we check with Google before crediting the coins or the Pro. We keep a record of every coin earned, bought and spent, and of what it bought.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account > Settings > Account > Delete account. Or email joshua.hermanto97@gmail.com from your account's address; we delete it within 30 days. Steps and details: Delete account. A farm owner can remove members at any time; an organisation's admin can remove its TS.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access is enforced on the server for every record, by the role each person holds.

Children

The app is for farm operators and their Technical Support (TS) and is not directed at children under 13.
