Menghapus akun Rekording Ayam

Di aplikasi

  1. Buka Lainnya > Pengaturan.
  2. Ketuk Hapus akun (paling bawah).
  3. Isi kata sandi Anda, ketuk Hapus akun saya, lalu pastikan sekali lagi.

Tanpa aplikasi

Kirim email ke joshua.hermanto97@gmail.com dari alamat email akun Anda, dengan judul "Hapus akun". Kami menghapusnya dalam 30 hari dan membalas bila sudah.

Yang dihapus

  • Akses masuk Anda (email dan kata sandi) dan nama Anda.
  • Keanggotaan Anda di setiap peternakan dan organisasi auditor.
  • Peternakan dan organisasi yang hanya Anda pakai: langsung ditutup, dan catatannya dihapus dari server kami dalam 30 hari.

Yang disimpan

  • Catatan koin dan pembelian (Google Play), sebagai catatan pembayaran.
  • Peternakan yang Anda pakai bersama tetapi bukan milik Anda tetap untuk timnya, termasuk catatan yang Anda isi di sana.
  • Nama Anda di kandang yang mencatat Anda sebagai PIC (penanggung jawab) di peternakan lain: tetap tercetak di laporan peternakan itu sebagai catatannya, tetapi tidak lagi terhubung ke akun Anda.

Peternakan milik Anda yang masih ada orang lain di dalamnya, atau organisasi buatan Anda yang masih ada auditornya, menahan penghapusan: keluarkan mereka dulu (Anggota, atau halaman organisasi), agar tidak ada tim yang kehilangan catatannya tanpa tahu.

Deleting your Rekording Ayam account

In the app

  1. Open More > Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account (at the bottom).
  3. Enter your password, tap Delete my account, then confirm once more.

Without the app

Email joshua.hermanto97@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password) and your name.
  • Your place on every farm and auditor organisation.
  • Farms and organisations only you used: closed at once, and their records erased from our servers within 30 days.

What is kept

  • The record of coins and purchases (Google Play), as payment records.
  • A farm you were on but did not own stays with its team, including the records you entered there.
  • Your name on a house that named you its PIC (person in charge) on another farm: it stays on that farm's reports as its record, no longer linked to your account.

A farm you own that others are still on, or an organisation you made that still has auditors, holds the deletion back: remove them first (People, or the organisation's page), so no team loses its records by surprise.
